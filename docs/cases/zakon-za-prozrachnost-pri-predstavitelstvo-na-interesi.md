## Lessons learnt

The act was promulgated in ДВ бр. 30 of 27 March 2026 (material 242221) and lex.bg prints its additional provision with no text under it, as it does for the transport law. Unlike the transport law, this act was amended in ДВ бр. 55 of 16 June 2026, so its promulgation material is no longer the whole act: the amendment is published as a list of changes, not as a new text, and the amending material does not carry the act's title in the Gazette index. Filling the dropped § 1 from the promulgated text is right only if the amendment did not touch § 1, and applying the amendment needs the engine that turns a Gazette material into changes to an act. That engine is step F1 of the plan and does not exist yet, so the route stopped at its first step without running anything. Nothing about the act was read or written in this run.

## Proposal

Leave the act on the list with the route "history" until the engine of F1 exists, then run the route again: the engine reads material 242221 as the whole act, attributes the amending material of бр. 55/2026 to it, applies the amendment and settles the differences from lex.bg and the portal. Until then the corpus keeps the lex.bg reading of 22 October 2026 with its dropped § 1, and the act counts as distance left. No ruling is needed now unless the owner wants the act handled before F1, in which case the only route that exists is a hand investigation of whether the amendment touched § 1, recorded as a case file; the plan does not provide for it.
